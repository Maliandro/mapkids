Privacy Policy

Map Kids (Meyade Games) is a game made for children.

  • We do not collect personal data. The explorer name, progress, settings and achievements stay on your device and are never sent to any server.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking.
  • Game Center is Apple's own service; if you sign in, achievements and scores are governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details. Purchase screens are behind a parental gate.
  • Deleting the app removes all game data from the device.
